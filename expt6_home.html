How these results were calculated

Completed live survey responses are grouped by the state and county selected in the survey. Each response contributes once. Donation totals sum the gift-card amounts participants chose to donate. Donor counts count positive donation choices. The map and county table use five-digit county FIPS codes; independent cities remain separate from similarly named counties.

State means are calculated from individual responses, weighted by the number of respondents in each county. Counties with respondents who all chose not to donate have a mean of $0 per respondent and no mean per donor. Counties without observations are not assigned a zero. Amounts and means are rounded to two decimal places for display.
